Travel inspirations

The place, then the piece

Cora makes the jewelry in Maine. Each trip below is shown the way she described it: a photograph from the road beside the piece it suggested. Guatemala and Brazil also supply stone. Greece, and Germany with the Netherlands, supply the idea.

Inspiration

Greece

Spirals, coils, and domes, found in museums and mountaintops.

Flat bronze spirals wound ring on ring, echoed in a hammered silver disc.

Bronze bracelets coiled and hung in rows, reimagined as hammered bangles wrapped in mixed metal.

A gold-trimmed shield with a meander border inspired these raised silver domes on chain.

Travel

Namibia and South Africa

Color, bone and bead, and a lot of sun.

Deadvlei, Namibia. Sun-baked wood against red dunes became long earrings of dark bone, wrapped in brass wire.

Cora, face to face with a python. Two gold snake heads meet at a gunmetal ring on a leather necklace.

Beads of the market. A bone bead necklace and earrings set, finished with brass accents.

Materials and color

Brazil

The earth gives the stones. The streets give the color, Copacabana.

A brass hand grips the door, answered in two clasped hands on a leather cord.

Broken tile pieced whole in color, echoed in raw amethyst mined in Brazil and strung with silver.

Amber beads and silver facets catching the light inspired this raw citrine, mined in Brazil, set with silver.

Materials

Guatemala

Jade, woven cloth and night sky, carried home in wire and stone. The painting is by Silvestre H.

Rough jade hearts at the workshop, answered in bag charms with wire-wrapped stones.

Life-size figures in a Descent from the Cross, echoed in teardrops wrapped in black with a silver accent.

Cora teaching a young artisan at the workshop, remembered in hammered half-moons with wire-wrapped jade.

Inspiration

Germany and the Netherlands

Walls, mirrors and masterpieces, turned into jewelry.

The Reichstag Dome folds the Berlin sky into one mirrored cone, echoed in a matte black diamond, a hammered copper ring, and a round black post.

A Guernica-style mural painted on the Berlin Wall, answered in mirrored line-art faces finished with curved brass posts.

Vermeer’s pearl on a banner over the Rijksmuseum entrance inspired real freshwater pearls and crystal rondelles on gold huggies.

Travel

Papua, Indonesia

Painted skin, carved wood, living reef.

Chalk-white rings and swirls painted on skin, answered in textured brass discs.

A carved river shield, tall and ovaled. Cream and dark shapes, stacked.

Reef coral folded in rings and beads. A beaded circle in brass.

Travel

Belize

Warm wood, gold light, and old stone.

Stacked stone temple steps at Altun Ha inspired these pyrite cube earrings, which stack the same way with gold beads between.

Peach and rose bougainvillea is echoed in hammered brass leaf earrings set with pearl and carnelian.

The warm wood and spices of a cooking class table come home in this bracelet of palm wood and gold-etched black beads.

Travel

India

Color, pattern and gold, carried home.

Elephants gathered at the water, echoed in antique brass elephant heads that close a leather necklace.

Foiled lampwork beads, so common across India, inspired this cairn earring design.

A painted ceiling in lapis, jade and gold, reimagined as teal glass over carved brass leaf discs.
