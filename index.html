Traveling the World for Inspiration and Materials

Handcrafted on the coast of Maine, using hammered metals, leather, freshwater pearls, semi-precious stones, druzies, and crystals.

Some countries supply the material. Guatemala is where Cora hand-picks jade. Brazil is a source for amethyst and citrine. Others, including Greece and Germany, supply the idea: a curve, a metal, a way of putting unlike things together.
