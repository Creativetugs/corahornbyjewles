Cora's story

Jewelry Inspired by Maine, Travel & Everyday Life

Cora Hornby's jewelry reflects a love of natural materials, unusual combinations, and beautiful things with a little personality.

Based in Cape Elizabeth, Maine, Cora draws inspiration from her seaside surroundings and travels around the world.

Her journey into jewelry design began with pieces created for family and friends. Over time, that creative work grew into a collection of handcrafted earrings, necklaces, bracelets and other jewelry.

Cora also works with partners in Guatemala, allowing her to source jade directly and hand-pick individual stones.
